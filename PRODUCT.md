# Adverting

## Register
brand-and-product

## Product Purpose
Daily-brief tool for digital marketing agencies and solo content marketers. Every morning on a schedule, the system pulls what is actually trending inside a chosen sector (food & bev, fitness, beauty, fintech, real estate, etc.), explains why each trend blew up, and delivers one concrete action per client for that day.

For agencies managing many clients, the product also ranks which client needs attention today, based on posting cadence, engagement vs. baseline, and how relevant today's trends are to that client's niche.

## Core Promise
Stop guessing what to post. Every morning, one specific script per client, built on what is actually winning in their sector this week.

## Users
- Solo content marketers (entry, 99 ₺/ay, 1 sector)
- Small and mid agencies managing 3 to 15 clients (main, 259 ₺/ay, 5 sectors)
- Larger agencies and in-house teams managing 15+ brands (enterprise, contact)

## Brand Tone
Specific, dated, declarative. Not aspirational. Numbers over adjectives. Verbs over adjectives. Short sentences. No emoji. No exclamation points. Confident, not desperate. Turkish-first locale, then English.

## Anti-References
- Vercel-template SaaS aesthetic. Inter / Poppins / Manrope. Purple-to-blue gradients. Glassmorphism. Sparkle wand icons. "AI-powered" badges. "Reimagine your..." copy. Square corners on interactive elements. Hero video of a hand holding a phone. Skeleton loaders.

## Strategic Principles
- The site itself is marketing creative. Reference: Dragon Rouge (dragonrouge.com) — confident typography as design, minimal nav, work on display.
- Anti-black-box. Every priority score, every learning bias, every input is inspectable and editable.
- The brief is just there. The user does not request it. Opening = reading, not loading.
- Mobile is the primary surface. Web companion is thin (billing, bulk import, PDF export, team admin).
- Three colors. Three fonts. No squares. No exceptions.

## Compliance
- KVKK / EU region only. All data in Frankfurt.
- Customer auth: phone + OTP. No Supabase Auth for end users.
- Multi-tenant: every table has org_id, RLS enforced.
